2.设备类型不符，不支持arm64
![](assets/板端调试/file-20260401103741838.png)
https://doc.embedfire.com/linux/rk356x/Ai/zh/latest/lubancat_ai/env/toolkit_lite2.html#id3
原因：缺少板端运行库librknnrt.so，同时发现runtime库和Toolkit2版本不匹配
~~~bash
(base) yuan@yuan-TpX13G2:~/BiYeSheJi/pt-rknn$ scp -r ./RKSDK/rknn-toolkit2-master/rknpu2/runtime/Linux/librknn_api/aarch64/ cat@lubancat.local:/home/cat/lib
~~~
参考此处https://www.cnblogs.com/sivon/p/19199909更新
~~~bash
cat@lubancat:~/Work/rknpu2/runtime/Linux$ strings /usr/bin/rknn_server | grep -i "rknn_server version"
rknn_server version: 2.3.2 (1842325 build@2025-03-30T09:54:34)
cat@lubancat:~/Work/rknpu2/runtime/Linux$ strings /usr/lib/librknnrt.so | grep -i "librknnrt version"
librknnrt version: 2.3.2 (429f97ae6b@2025-04-09T09:09:27)
~~~
输出版本一致成功。
